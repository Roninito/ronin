# Market Solution Workflow

## Purpose

Take a shipped feature or app and get it in front of an audience, then prove
it happened. "Done" means: the announcement is live in at least two places,
every post has a verified screenshot, and a report with those screenshots
has been emailed out.

## When to use

Reach for this whenever a duty or the user is asked to "market", "launch",
"announce", or "promote" something that's already built and ready to ship —
not for planning or building the thing itself, only the push to get it seen.

## Standards & expectations

- Never claim a post succeeded without a screenshot to back it up.
- Tone: confident, specific, no hype-speak ("revolutionary", "game-changing").
- Always link back to the primary destination (site/app) from every post.
- If a post fails, note the failure in the report rather than skipping it
  silently.

## Steps

1. Post the announcement to Twitter (twitter skill).
2. Screenshot the resulting post (agent-browser skill) — this is the proof
   the post went live, and gets reused in step 3 and the final report.
3. Reuse that screenshot on the secondary site/post (e.g. a blog embed or a
   second platform), crediting the original post.
4. Publish the secondary post.
5. Screenshot the secondary post as well.
6. Compile all screenshots + links into a short report and email it
   (email-manager) — recipient and cadence depend on context; ask if unclear.

## Notes

Adjust freely as real launches reveal what actually matters — e.g. which
